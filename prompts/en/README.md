# Prompts in English

One prompt per school of psychology, plus one that compares them. Each prompt explains how a school would read your situation and never diagnoses or labels anyone.

- [All lenses, compared](all-lenses.md)
- [Psychodynamic](psychodynamic.md): Hidden motives and feelings, and old relationship patterns that replay in the present.
- [Jungian](jungian.md): What we project onto others, the roles we wear, and what the situation asks us to grow into.
- [Cognitive behavioural (CBT)](cbt.md): The link between what happened, what you told yourself about it, what you felt and what you did.
- [Attachment theory](attachment.md): The need for closeness and safety, and what each person does when the connection feels threatened.
- [Family systems](family-systems.md): The whole system of relationships: roles, alliances, who gets pulled in, and patterns passed down the generations.
- [Transactional analysis](transactional-analysis.md): Which inner 'state' each person speaks from, and repeating 'games' with predictable endings.
- [Gottman couples research](gottman.md): How a couple's conversation goes: how it starts, which harmful patterns appear, and whether repair happens.
- [Nonviolent communication](nvc.md): The difference between what happened and how we judge it, and the feelings, needs and requests behind words.
